# Product Discovery

## Project

AI Legal Document Reviewer


## Problem Statement

Individuals, startups, and small businesses frequently review legal documents such as NDAs, employment contracts, and service agreements without access to affordable legal expertise. These documents are often lengthy, use complex legal language, and make it difficult to identify key obligations, risks, or missing clauses. As a result, users spend significant time reviewing documents, risk overlooking important terms, or incur high legal consultation costs.


## Target Users


1. Small business owners reviewing contracts without dedicated legal teams.

2. Individuals reviewing employment contracts, rental agreements, NDAs, and other legal documents.

3. Legal professionals seeking AI-assisted document review to improve productivity.


## User Pain Points

1. Legal documents are difficult to understand due to complex legal language.

2. Reviewing contracts manually is time-consuming and increases the risk of overlooking important clauses or obligations.

3. Accessing professional legal advice for every document can be expensive, especially for individuals and small businesses.

4. Existing AI tools often provide answers without reliable references, making users question their accuracy and trustworthiness.

5. Current document review workflows require switching between multiple tools, reducing productivity.


## Opportunity

The rapid adoption of Generative AI has created an opportunity to make legal document review faster, more accessible, and more affordable. By combining AI with document retrieval and grounded responses, the product can help individuals, small businesses, and legal professionals reduce review time, lower legal costs, and make more informed decisions while increasing confidence in AI-assisted legal analysis.

## Proposed Solution

Develop an AI-powered legal document review platform that enables users to upload and review legal documents, receive plain-language summaries, identify potential risks and missing clauses, and ask questions through a conversational interface. The platform will integrate with commonly used document management and editing tools to fit naturally into existing legal workflows, while providing responses with citations to improve transparency and trust.

## Success Metrics

- Reduce the average time spent reviewing legal documents.
- Increase average user trust in AI-generated document analysis.
- Increase the number of legal documents reviewed per user.
- Improve users' understanding of legal documents through AI-generated summaries.

### In Scope

- Upload legal documents for AI analysis.
- Generate plain-language summaries and identify potential risks or missing clauses.
- Interact with uploaded documents through a conversational AI assistant.
- Provide document-grounded responses with citations to support AI-generated insights.
