# Market Research

## Market Overview

The product operates within the Legal Technology (LegalTech) market, specifically focusing on AI-assisted legal document review. Recent advances in Generative AI and Large Language Models have enabled faster and more accessible analysis of complex legal documents. Businesses, legal professionals, and individuals increasingly seek cost-effective solutions to review contracts, understand legal obligations, and reduce the time required for document analysis, creating a strong opportunity for AI-powered legal review platforms.


## Industry Trends

1. The LegalTech market is experiencing rapid growth, driven by increasing investment in Generative AI and document automation technologies.

2. Law firms and corporate legal departments are rapidly adopting Generative AI to improve productivity, reduce manual effort, and accelerate legal workflows.

3. Organisations increasingly demand trustworthy AI solutions that provide accurate, explainable, and document-grounded responses, creating opportunities for AI-powered legal document review platforms.

## Market Gap

Current AI legal solutions primarily target enterprise customers, law firms, and legal professionals with premium pricing and feature-rich platforms. This leaves individuals, startups, and small businesses underserved, as they often require affordable, easy-to-use tools for understanding and reviewing legal documents without specialised legal expertise. There is an opportunity to build a self-service AI legal document reviewer that prioritises simplicity, accessibility, transparent pricing, and trustworthy AI-powered analysis for non-enterprise users.


## Target Positioning

The AI Legal Document Reviewer is positioned as an affordable, AI-powered legal document review platform for individuals, startups, and small businesses. Unlike enterprise-focused legal AI solutions, the product prioritises simplicity, self-service onboarding, and plain-language legal insights while providing trustworthy, document-grounded analysis with citations.

## Key Takeaways

- The LegalTech market is experiencing rapid growth, driven by increasing adoption of Generative AI.
- Existing AI legal platforms primarily target enterprise customers, law firms, and legal professionals.
- Individuals, startups, and small businesses remain underserved due to high pricing, complex workflows, and enterprise-focused product design.
- There is a clear opportunity to build an affordable, self-service AI legal document reviewer that delivers trustworthy, document-grounded legal insights for non-expert users.
